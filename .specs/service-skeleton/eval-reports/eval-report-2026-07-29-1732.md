## 1. Each AC is testable — **PASS**

The full walk found 57 ACs and an observable decision procedure for each. HTTP criteria specify

named internal details. Operational criteria are equally observable: AC9.4 requires documented
probes to report `UP`, AC9.5 distinguishes readiness from liveness during database loss, and
AC11.1 names the exact build lifecycle and check classes.
